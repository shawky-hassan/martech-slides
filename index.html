What CDP actually is

Simple view

CDP is the layer that sits between your source systems and your activation tools.

  • Ingests data from CRM, Marketo, Sales Cloud, Outreach, MDG, and other sources.
  • Resolves duplicate identities into one unified customer profile.
  • Sends segments and triggers to downstream channels.
Why it matters to you

It gives marketing and ABX one trusted audience layer instead of working separately in each tool.

  • Marketo becomes an email activation destination, not the system of truth.
  • Salesforce provides CRM facts and opportunity context.
  • Demandbase can become an intelligence input layer for segmentation and buying-group motion.

The core CDP flow

Collect data → unify it → decide who matters → activate it.

The objects that matter most

Four concepts you need to understand before building any exploration.

Unified Customer Profile

The golden-record view used for segmentation. CDP decides who a person or account really is after merging all source-level contributors.

Contextual Profile

The source-level contributor view. Shows the individual records from each system (Salesforce, Marketo, etc.) that feed the unified profile.

Indicators

The KPI engine. Activity, calculated, predictive, and recurring-date indicators are the trusted quantitative inputs for segmentation — use these, not raw activity fields.

Segments

The classification layer — Gold / Silver / Platinum or Low / Medium / High interest — based on indicator thresholds and business rules.

Rule to remember: always use pre-built calculated indicators in explorations, not raw activity counts — indicators are the trusted exact logic.

How a real use case would work

The account → contact cross-profile pattern — the most important blueprint from your training.

Step 1

Start with ICP / target universe

Choose the right account universe first. For Concur, this becomes the mandatory starting point once ICPs are in CDP.

Step 2

Filter accounts

Region, industry, status, opportunity exclusions, engagement model, buying-group conditions.

Step 3

Switch to contacts

Add buyer roles, relationship type, email eligibility, and direct marketing consent.

Step 4

Activate

Push as batch audience to Marketo, or trigger a CX Flow in real time when the condition is met.
